Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

ClearTake does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Your videos and audio are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded.

No data collection

The app does not collect any personal information. There is no account or sign-in, and we have no access to who you are, where you are, or how you use the app.

Your videos and audio

Noise removal, voice EQ and volume adjustments run locally on your iPhone or iPad. Files you open are copied into the app's temporary storage only while you edit them; iOS clears this storage automatically.

Enhanced files are saved only where you choose — your photo library, the Files app, or another app you share to. ClearTake never modifies your original video.

Permissions

  • Choosing videos uses the system photo picker, which gives the app access only to the items you select.
  • Saving to Photos asks for add-only access so the app can save enhanced videos. It cannot read the rest of your library.
  • Files are opened and saved through the system document picker.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

Purchases

The one-time Premium purchase is processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device, and the count of free exports is stored only on your device.

Analytics and third parties

We do not use any analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks. The app contains no trackers of any kind. Noise removal is powered by the open-source DeepFilterNet model, which runs entirely on your device.

Children's privacy

ClearTake does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be communicated through an app update.
